# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and experiences. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help keep its spaces respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on the people behind them.
- Respect differing opinions and experience levels.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not publish private or identifying information without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberate intimidation.
- Insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualized language or attention that is unwelcome.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or private communications without consent.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a project community.

These expectations apply in the repository, its issues and discussions, pull requests, and other spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove content or take other action appropriate to the behavior, including restricting participation in project spaces.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/lbenicio). Do not post private reports or sensitive personal information in public issues or discussions. The maintainer will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible and will respect the reporter's privacy to the extent practical.
